What are they?

Flowers, also known as a bloom, are the reproductive structures of flowering plants. They are often in bright unique colors which fascinate humans. It is a common practice to cultivate flowers for their beauty and fragrance. Since it offers a huge diversity where many unique characteristics can be seen, it is a popular and common gift in our society. Different kind of flowers holds different meanings which is important to know in order to gift the correct flower to one person, depending on their importance in your life.

These blossoms are also often romanticized because of its color. When life gets difficult, we often look at flowers to brighten our day. In a dark and grey world, flowers color our lives and make us feel alive.
