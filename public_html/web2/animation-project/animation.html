The Hamburger Method

In fashion, you will most likely love someones outfit and wonder how they were able to make it so balanced and nice. Well in fashion, there is this method that many likes to use which is called the Hamburger Method. This way of dressing up requires the top and bottom element to match. For example, if you wear a red hat, you should pair it with red shoes or also if you're wearing a grey shirt, it would be good if your shoes are also grey. This method helps your outfits feel more balanced and equal. It helps the outfit feel more complete since the eye looks from the top to bottom.
